# Flight and long-range teleport spells

Players only get spells from the skill trees: spell books, scrolls and manuscripts are forbidden. So a spell taken off every tree is gone for players. Short blinks stay, as asked; Teleport and Thunderstep are the reference.

## Removed from the trees

| Spell | Mod | What it does | Was in | Replaced by |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Angel Wings | Iron's Spells | Wings that act as an elytra (flight) | Holy base tree | Fortify |
| Ascension | Iron's Spells | Shoots you into the air with reduced gravity (flight) | Lightning base tree | Thunder Cloud |
| Magic Cloud (cloud_ride) | Magic from the East | A rideable flying cloud | Jade base tree | Devastating Cut |
| Winged Ruin | Soma's Spells | Wings: you rise and dive onto a chosen point (flight) | Jade Archer (Jade + Stalker), Spirit Blade (Samurai + Jade) | Magic Glintblade / Spiral of Ruin |

## Already in no tree (players can't get them)

| Spell | Mod | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Portal | Iron's Spells | Two linked portals, any distance (long-range teleport) |
| Recall | Iron's Spells | Warps you to your spawn point (long-range teleport) |
| Seraph Embodiment | MDVLCraft | Slow flight as a seraph. Built, but in no tree |
| Device: Array | Esoteric Spells | Its devices teleport, not the caster |

## Kept, please double check

| Spell | Mod | Range / why kept | In |
|---|---|---|---|
| Teleport | Iron's Spells | The short reference teleport you named | Thief |
| Thunderstep | Iron's Spells | Short forward teleport you named (Iron's lists it as invalid for trees, so it's in none) | none |
| Blood Step | Iron's Spells | Same range as Teleport (to where you look, or behind a target) | Assassin and others |
| Frost Step | Iron's Spells | Same range as Teleport | Frost |
| Blink | Traveler's Optics | Short, low-cost blink | Samurai and others |
| Blight Step | Cursed School | Short blink | Cursed trees |
| Chrono Step | Chronomancy | Short step along your aim | Chronomancy trees |
| Esoteric Step | Esoteric Spells | Teleports toward your aim, capped at the spell's maximum range (similar to Teleport) | Wanderer |
| Spectral Blink | Traveler's Optics | For 3 s you can teleport to a creature you look at, even through walls. Range is the look distance, so it can be long. Candidate for removal | Assassin |
| Evasion | Iron's Spells | Short random dodge-teleport when hit | Thief, Wanderer |
| Vortex Uppercut | Traveler's Optics | Teleports into its own vortex a short way ahead | none |
| Wind Jump, Aeropic, Tailwind | Wind Spellbooks | Jumps and launches, not sustained flight | Several trees |
| Demonic Assimilation | Anti-Magic | Its flight is already switched off by the binder (no mid-air jump toggle) | Berserker |
| End's Mayhem | Alshanex's Familiars | "Ruler of the End" while channelling; no flight found in its code path | Warlock |

Gate walls also stop any teleport or ability that would move a player through a wall inside a dungeon (`gate/GateProtection`).
