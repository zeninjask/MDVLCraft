# MDVLCraft skill trees

Generated by `tools/generate_skill_trees.py`; edit the script, not this file.

Layout follows the radial style of the *RPG skill tree* and *More RPG Classes - Skill Tree* packs.

- **Classes tab:** the four classes sit in a ring round the centre and are unlocked automatically. Their archetypes fan out in each class's quarter. Pick up to **2 archetypes** from any classes; each opens its own tab.
- **Archetype tabs:** the archetype skill sits in the centre and spokes radiate out. Each bonus spoke is a chain of small 1-point bonuses that ends in a 2-point capstone. Ability spokes unlock spells or techniques for the ability wheel.
- **Points:** 1 to start, then 1 per level up to level 40 (41 per archetype). Every tree costs more than that, so you choose. Experience comes from kills (`dropped XP + max health / 5`, at most 20 kills per chunk every 5 minutes). Level n to n+1 costs `25 + 5n` XP (4,900 XP to reach level 40).
- **Class bonuses** are shared by every archetype of that class. **Signature bonuses and abilities** belong to one archetype only; the generator enforces this.
- **Controls:** `[` opens the abilities screen, hold `` ` `` for the ability wheel, Caps Lock casts. Cursed techniques cost mana and have no cooldown.
