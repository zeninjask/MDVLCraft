# MDVLCraft skill trees

Generated by `tools/generate_skill_trees.py`; edit the script, not this file.

- **Classes tab:** the four classes are unlocked automatically. Pick up to **2 archetypes** (any classes). Each opens its own tab.
- **Archetype tabs:** 1 starting point, then 1 point per level up to level 20 (21 points). Experience comes from kills: `dropped XP + max health / 5`, at most 20 kills per chunk every 5 minutes. Level n to n+1 costs `40 + 15n` XP (3,650 XP to reach level 20).
- **No overlap:** every attribute and every spell or technique belongs to exactly one archetype (the generator enforces this).
- **Abilities** go on the 8-slot ability wheel: `[` opens the abilities screen, hold `` ` `` for the wheel, Caps Lock casts. Cursed techniques cost mana and have no cooldown.
