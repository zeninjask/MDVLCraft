# MDVLCraft skill trees

Generated by `tools/generate_skill_trees.py`; edit the script, not this file.

Layout follows the radial style of the *RPG skill tree* and *More RPG Classes - Skill Tree* packs.

- **Classes tab:** the four classes sit in a ring round the centre and are unlocked automatically. Their archetypes fan out in each class's quarter; hover one for its full description and key abilities. Pick up to **2 archetypes** from any classes; each opens its own tab.
- **Archetype tabs:** the archetype skill sits at the root of a tree. A few boughs leave it at uneven angles and fork into limbs; every tree has its own shape and size, and branches never cross. Each bonus limb is a chain of small 1-point bonuses that ends in a 2-point capstone (shield frame). Ability limbs (star frames) unlock spells or techniques for the ability wheel.
- **Twigs:** the small round studs sprouting off every branch are tiny 1-point generic bonuses (health, speed, mana, cooldowns, saturation and more; table below). They are smaller than any class or signature step and capped per tree, so they widen your choices without raising the ceiling.
- **Points:** 1 to start, then 1 per level up to level 40 (41 per archetype). Every tree costs more than that, so you choose. Experience comes from kills (`dropped XP + max health / 5`, at most 20 kills per chunk every 5 minutes). Level n to n+1 costs `25 + 5n` XP (4,900 XP to reach level 40).
- **Class bonuses** are shared by every archetype of that class. **Signature bonuses and abilities** belong to one archetype only; the generator enforces this.
- **Controls:** `[` opens the abilities screen, hold `` ` `` for the ability wheel, Caps Lock casts. Cursed techniques cost mana and have no cooldown; hold the cast key on Dismantle or Cleave to keep casting, and cast the Malevolent Shrine again to close it (it lasts up to 20 s).
