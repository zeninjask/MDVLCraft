# MDVLCraft Binder

> **Source status (0.7.8):** `src/` was recovered by decompiling the binder 0.7.8 jar from the 1.9.19 packs (renamed to Mojang names, then Vineflower) and rebuilds to the same classes (every method signature identical; the few bytecode differences are only reordered branches). Code comments from the original were lost. `tools/generate_skill_trees.py` predates 0.7.8 and refuses to run (it does not define the Monk and Water trees), and `SKILL_TREES.md`, `TREE_TOTALS.md` and `BALANCE.md` describe 0.7.1; the tree data in `src/main/resources/data/mdvlcraft/puffish_skills` is the 0.7.8 data.

The pack's own mod (Forge 1.20.1 / 47.4.10, Java 17). It:

1. **Keeps Iron's Spells and Cursed Fate spells and techniques inside the skill trees.** Their items, recipes, ores, structures (with their mobs and loot) are in the game. What's kept out is anything that hands out spells or traits on its own: spell books, scrolls and eldritch manuscripts (in any loot, drop or trade), the scroll forge and inscription table, Cursed Fate's cursed shards, Epic Fight skill books, and Iron's own casting from items (staffs, Magic Blade). Their natural mob spawns, keybinds, menus and advancements stay off. Spells, techniques and Epic Fight skills come only from the skill trees.
2. **Adds vocation skill trees** (Pufferfish's Skills). On the **Archetypes** page you pick two of 18 archetypes (three classes, Warrior, Mage and Rogue, six each); the pair is your vocation (153 of them, each with its own name and title) and opens one vocation tab: the first archetype's base tree on the left, the second's mirrored on the right, and the vocation's own skills and bonuses in a centre column from its root. The tabs are composed when the data pack loads from the parts in `puffish_skills/vocation_parts/` (`skills/VocationParts`, `mixin/puffish/PackConfigReaderMixin`), written by `tools/generate_vocations.py` from the design in `tools/vocations/`. Modified abilities that are not built yet are `mdvlcraft:planned_<key>` (`ability/PlannedAbility`, `data/mdvlcraft/planned_abilities.json`): they have an icon, name and description and sit on the wheel, but casting one only says it is not built yet. Players who picked archetypes under the old Classes page get a pop-up on joining to pick two archetypes, and their new vocation starts at the level of their highest old archetype (`skills/LegacyMigration`, `client/MigrationPopup`). A respec picks both archetypes again and starts the new vocation at three quarters of the old level, with every point to spend again (`skills/Respec`). The older archetype trees are described in [`SKILL_TREES.md`](SKILL_TREES.md); ideas for moving spells and adding archetypes are in [`SPELL_REPORT.md`](SPELL_REPORT.md), and the class improvements (now applied) in [`CLASS_IMPROVEMENTS.md`](CLASS_IMPROVEMENTS.md).
3. **Makes every weapon work with Epic Fight and Ponder.** Weapons that only had Epic Fight's generic fallback get the type that fits them (`data/*/capabilities/weapons/`), and Ponder (hold W over an item) gets a scene for every Epic Fight weapon type, not just Epic Fight's own eight (`client/ponder/`). `/mdvl weapons` writes a report of every weapon and its Epic Fight type.
4. **Epic Fight × Iron's compatibility.** The pack adds *Epic Fight x Iron's Spells: Enhanced Animations* (casting animations in Epic Fight mode; Iron's first-person arms and items are turned off as it requires), and the binder gives Iron's summoned zombies, skeletons and vexes Epic Fight's mob behaviour (`data/irons_spellbooks/epicfight_mobpatch/`).
5. **Adds the ability wheel.** Spells, techniques (Cursed Fate, Heavenly Restriction, Projection Sorcery, Nightfall's Soul Hunt) and the Samurai's Stance unlocked in the trees go on an 8-slot wheel. Spells cost 40% less mana than Iron's defaults and have 50% longer cooldowns (Iron's per-spell config, written by the tree generator). Techniques cost mana and each has its own cooldown (`ability/TechniqueCooldowns`), shaded on the wheel and HUD like a spell's. See [`BALANCE.md`](BALANCE.md).
   - **Stance (Samurai).** Cast it to take a stance up or drop it; sneak and cast to change element (an active stance changes with it). One element at a time: **Fire** +15% attack speed, **Wind** +40% dodge distance, **Water** +50% perfect-parry window (Epic Fight's Parrying guard), **Earth** +15% attack damage. Your weapon swing trails take the element's colour (orange, pale green, blue, amber), which nearby players see too. The wheel icon shows your element and is dimmed while the stance is off. Free; it is off again after logging in or respawning (`ability/Stances`, `StanceElement`, `mixin/epicfight/`).
6. **Teaches Epic Fight skills through the skill trees.** 21 of Epic Fight's dodge, guard, mobility, identity and passive skills sit in the archetype trees (star frames with the skill's icon), each in one archetype only: 6 for Warrior archetypes, 5 each for Mage, Rogue and Ranger (list in [`SKILL_TREES.md`](SKILL_TREES.md)). Unlocking the node teaches the skill; locking it (or erasing the tab) unequips and forgets it. Everyone knows Roll and Guard, which fill empty Dodge and Guard slots. Skills are equipped in the abilities screen's **Combat Skills** box; Epic Fight's own skill screen and its key are gone, and its equip packet is ignored because it would equip any skill the client names (`epicskill/`, `mixin/epicfight/CPChangeSkillMixin`, `EpicFightKeyMappingsMixin`). The *Epic Fight: Skill Tree* mod is no longer in the pack.
7. **Explains shipbuilding with Ponder** (aleki's Nifty Ships has only a Patchouli book, and Patchouli is not in the pack): hold W over a watercraft frame, oarlock, oar, cleat or anchor for *Building a Rowboat* and *Building a Sloop* (`client/ponder/ShipPonderPlugin`, `ShipPonderScenes`; plates in `assets/mdvlcraft/ponder/`).
8. **Gives every menu the Dragon Quest IX look:** the pack's font is the DQ9 pixel font (`tools/generate_font.py` turns EvilAsh25's Fontstruct font, CC BY-NC-SA 3.0, into a bitmap font over Minecraft's default; licence in `tools/font/`), and windows are black or charcoal with the game's rounded light grey, white and dark grey bevel, with a thick blue frame for what is selected (`client/DqGui`). The binder's screens (menu, status page, abilities, combat skills, wheel, respec), every vanilla or Forge text button and slider (`mixin/AbstractButtonMixin`, `AbstractSliderButtonMixin`), tooltips and the menu backgrounds use it; the skill screen's window, tabs and canvas are drawn by `tools/generate_textures.py` (`mixin/SkillsScreenMixin`); Every container screen (inventory, chests, furnaces, crafting, villagers, creative tabs, recipe book), JEI's panels, Villager Recruits, the Origins pickers, toasts and other mods' vanilla-grey GUI textures are recoloured by `tools/generate_dq_skins.py` into the built-in "MDVLCraft DQ UI" resource pack, and their dark labels are drawn white (`client/DqSkins`, `mixin/GuiGraphicsMixin`, `ToastComponentMixin`); the HUD and icon sheets are left alone, and the ability HUD keeps its Slate look (`client/SlateGui`). The top-left party HUD is the game's party status windows (`client/PartyPanels`): you first, then each party member by name, with name, HP and MP bars, the emblems of their archetypes in two vocation boxes (the higher level on the left) and that archetype's level (`skills/ArchetypeLevels` sends everyone's to every client); other members' HP and MP are the ones Parties syncs, and Parties' own HUD frames are hidden (its world trackers stay). Every window widens alike to fit the longest name. When anyone's top archetype gains experience, a small window under theirs shows it Pokemon style (`client/ExpBars`): the bar fills towards the next level (emptying and refilling on a level up) with "+N" counting up beneath; another gain while it fills carries the bar on and adds to N. The survival inventory is the game's equipment screen (`client/DqInventoryScreen`): the bag, the 2x2 crafting grid and the player's stats on the left; the 3D player model, one row per equipment slot (main hand, off hand, head, body, legs, feet: the item's name with a green E, or "Nothing equipped") and the Curios accessories on the right. It opens Curios' own inventory menu, which already handles the slots, crafting and syncing, and lays its slots out by what they hold (`mixin/SlotAccessor`); the player's effects are listed in an Effects window left of the bag (icon, name and time left; only icons, with the name and time on hover, when the screen has no room for names), which JEI's bookmarks keep clear of (`compat/JeiInventoryEffects`); in creative mode a Creative button in the Equipment title goes to the creative menu, and the creative menu has an Inventory button back to this screen (`DqInventoryScreen.onScreenInit`); The accessories are head, necklace, two rings, talisman and belt: the binder takes four ring slots and the cloak and charm slots away (`data/mdvlcraft/curios/slots/`, Curios' REMOVE size operation; anything worn there goes back to the inventory). The belt holds a lantern and nothing else, and a lantern worn there lights up its wearer like a held one, for everyone nearby (`content/LanternBelt`, `mixin/curios/CuriosImplMixinHooksMixin`, `mixin/dynamiclights/LanternBeltLightMixin`, counted with the held items by Sodium Dynamic Lights, whose entity light option the pack leaves off). Inventory Profiles Next's sort buttons sit on the Bag and Craft titles and its profile selector above the windows (`@IPNGuiHints`). Every binder icon (abilities, Epic Fight skills, bonuses, tabs, archetypes, origins and stances) is a Dragon Quest IX item icon at the game's 32 x 32, picked by meaning by `tools/dq9_icons.py` (mapping in `tools/dq9_icons.json`). They are Square Enix's artwork, taken from the Dragon Quest Wiki, so they may only ship in this private pack. The pack's default GUI scale is 3.

## What's kept out, in code
`content/DisabledContent` decides: **forbidden items** are anything implementing Iron's `ISpellbook` or `IScroll` (any mod's spell books and scrolls), eldritch manuscripts, the scroll forge, the inscription table, `cursedfate:cursed_shards` and Epic Fight skill books (`SkillBookItem`, any mod's). **Disabled systems** are those of the `irons_spellbooks` and `cursedfate` namespaces.

| What | How |
|---|---|
| Forbidden items in creative tabs | `content/ContentEvents` (the pack has no recipe viewer since JEI was removed in 1.8.0) |
| Recipes | `mixin/RecipeManagerMixin`: recipes that make a forbidden item are not loaded; every other recipe from both mods is |
| Loot (chests, mob and block drops, fishing, global loot modifiers) | `mixin/ForgeHooksMixin` strips forbidden items from every generated loot roll; `ContentEvents.onLivingDrops` covers code drops |
| Trades | `ContentEvents.onEntityInteract`: any merchant (villagers, wandering trader, Iron's priests and wizards) drops offers involving a forbidden item before trading |
| Scroll forge and inscription table placed by structures | `ContentEvents.onRightClickBlock`: right-clicking them does nothing |
| Iron's own casting | `mixin/irons/SpellSelectionManagerMixin`: Iron's "selected spell" (from spell books, held items, armour and curios) is always empty, so staffs, casting implements and Epic Fight Skill - ISS's Magic Blade cast nothing and the spells built into Iron's unique weapons stay unreachable; staffs and gear keep their spell power |
| Natural spawns | `content/StripDisabledBiomeModifier` (biome modifier `mdvlcraft:strip_disabled`) and `ContentEvents.onFinalizeSpawn` (NATURAL spawn reason). Structure, spawner, spell and command spawns are allowed |
| Keybinds | `mixin/irons/KeyMappingsMixin`, `mixin/cursedfate/CursedfateModKeyMappingsMixin`, `mixin/epicfight/EpicFightKeyMappingsMixin` (Epic Fight's skill screen key only): unbound and kept out of the Controls screen |
| Epic Fight's skill equip packet | `mixin/epicfight/CPChangeSkillMixin`: ignored. Equipping goes through the binder's `EquipSkillPacket`, which checks the skill is learned and fits the slot |
| Cursed Fate menus, combat mode, abilities, quests, dash and roll | `mixin/NetworkHooksMixin`: the server drops every client-to-server packet on Cursed Fate's channels; `client/ClientContentSetup` removes the double-tap-Ctrl dash |
| Cursed Fate progression | `content/DisabledContentSetup` unregisters its listeners for random techniques on join, level/mastery XP (levels raise max health), Sukuna's fingers added to opened chests, quests, the random Reverse Curse Technique unlock, and an attacker set that is never cleared. Its mob drops stay (cloth and residue for its recipes) |
| Advancements | `mixin/ServerAdvancementManagerMixin` removes their display (no toasts or tabs). They are kept because Cursed Fate grants one by ID on join |

Iron's mana, cast-bar, recast and screen-effect overlays stay because the wheel's spells use them. Its spell bar and spell wheel need a spellbook or its keys, so they never show.

## Editing the skill trees
Edit `tools/generate_skill_trees.py`, then run `python3 tools/generate_skill_trees.py` from this folder. Bonuses use vanilla, Forge, Epic Fight, Iron's, Pufferfish's Attributes and `mdvlcraft:` attributes. The script fails if:
- a class bonus is used by two classes, or a signature bonus or ability by two archetypes (or also as a class bonus). Twig bonuses (`GENERIC`) are shared on purpose and capped per tree;
- a spell level is above Iron's maximum, or a granted spell costs more than the 100-mana starting pool;
- a limb (class, signature or ability skill) finds no room to grow, or two skills overlap on screen.

Each tree's shape comes from a random generator seeded with the archetype's ID, so it stays the same between runs; `TREE_SIZE` and `TREE_SIZE_SPREAD` set how big trees grow.

The reward format for any Pufferfish tree is:
```json
{"type": "mdvlcraft:ability", "data": {"ability": "irons_spellbooks:shield", "level": 2}}
```
Techniques use `mdvlcraft:dismantle`, `mdvlcraft:cleave`, `mdvlcraft:malevolent_shrine`, `mdvlcraft:open_malevolent_shrine`, `mdvlcraft:boogie_woogie`, `mdvlcraft:block_clap`, `mdvlcraft:blitz`, `mdvlcraft:surprise_attack`, `mdvlcraft:follow_up_kick`, `mdvlcraft:projection_dash` and `mdvlcraft:soul_hunt`, at level 1.
Epic Fight skills (any learnable dodge, guard, mobility, identity or passive skill) use:
```json
{"type": "mdvlcraft:epicfight_skill", "data": {"skill": "epicfight:berserker"}}
```

## Operator commands
`/mdvl abilities <player>` lists unlocked abilities, the wheel and mana. `/mdvl assign <player> <slot> <ability>`, `/mdvl clear <player> <slot>`, `/mdvl cast <player> <slot>` and `/mdvl release <player>` do what the client keys do. Players change vocation from the Respec page of the M menu. Pufferfish's `/puffish_skills skills lock <player> mdvlcraft:archetypes <archetype>` takes one archetype away and erases the vocation tab.

## Build and run
| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `./gradlew build` | `build/libs/mdvlcraft-<version>.jar` |
| `./gradlew runClient` / `runServer` | Dev client or server with the binder and the mods it compiles against |

Requires JDK 17. The jar goes in the `mods` folder on the client **and** the server. Hard dependencies (mods.toml): Epic Fight, Iron's Spells, Cataclysm, Pufferfish's Skills, Pufferfish's Attributes, Cursed Fate, EpicFight-Ponder, aleki's Nifty Ships, ISS Ponder, Sword Soaring, Balda's Projection Sorcery, Epic Fight Nightfall, Distant Horizons, Chunky.

`data/irons_spellbooks/irons_spellbooks_spell_config/` (generated) sets every granted spell's mana multiplier (0.6) and cooldown; edit `SPELL_MANA_MULTIPLIER` and the cooldown rule in the generator, not the files.

To compile against another pack mod, add `<name>_cf=<projectId>:<fileId>` (from the pack manifest) to `gradle.properties` and `implementation fg.deobf(cf('<name>'))` to `build.gradle`. Client-only mixins go in the `client` list of `mdvlcraft.mixins.json`.
