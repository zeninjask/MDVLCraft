# Archetype ranking (1.9.22, clean slate)

Ranked from the live tree data after the 1.9.22 additions (more health for Rogue/Ranger/Monk, 67 new abilities, Clash
Blade for everyone), without reference to earlier rankings. A full tree is assumed (no level cap, so every tree fills in
time) and solo PvE against this pack's mobs (health x1.2, armour x0.65 enchant reduction, Cataclysm bosses).

What counts, in order: how hard the archetype is to kill (health, armour, damage/magic resistance, healing, life steal,
dodges), how much damage it puts out (spell count and quality, weapon damage, attack damage), and what else the kit
gives (mobility, summons, control). Spell counts matter less than their quality: one Black Hole beats five utility
spells.

## Recommendations

### Too strong (keep the game hard)

1. **Mage Hunter.** Cut damage resistance 38% → 20% and magic resistance 25% → 15%, or drop Hyper Vitality and
   Endurance from its tree. Give Anti-Magic Shroud and Black Ward cooldowns of 90 s or more.
2. **Knight.** Damage resistance 38% → 30%. Auto Lances Magic cooldown 1.5× (it fires on its own).
3. **Samurai.** Damage reflection 25% → 15%; Fatal Flash stays (it only kills low-health targets).
4. **Warlock.** Keep its burst but make it pay: Black Hole 216 mana and a cooldown of 120 s or more, Supernova stays at
   300 mana / 180 s. Do not give it more health.
5. **Clash Blade for everyone** cancels melee hits and projectiles mid-swing. That is a large free defence for every
   class. To keep fights hard, either raise its stamina cost / cooldown in its config, or raise mob damage by about 10%.
6. **More health for Rogue and Ranger** (+3 to +6 health each) closes the gap to the mages. Mobs could take +10%
   health to compensate.

### Too weak

1. **Stalker.** Give it +30% arrow penetration or bow damage on its twigs, and raise max mana to about 160 so its
   13 spells are usable. Swap Arrow Tenacity for an active skill.
2. **Assassin.** Raise dagger and backstab damage (combined +1.85 → about +3.5), or move its spells to a school it can
   scale (blood or ender).
3. **Scout.** Add spell power for its school, or a ranged weapon bonus, so its new summons and Stele Cascade hit.
4. **Monk.** Give it 15–20% damage resistance or cooldown reduction on techniques, since it has almost no spells.
5. **Lightning.** Raise health 7.3 → 9 to match the other elemental mages.
6. **Thief.** Raise max mana 130 → about 180 so the new spells can actually be cast; keep its armour low.

### Watch list

- **Ice** has the most spells (26) and 42% magic resistance. Fine now, but do not add more to it.
- **Necromancy** summons scale with the summon damage attribute. If summons start clearing bosses alone, trim that
  first.
- **Thief** at 24 spells is close to a mage kit on a rogue. If the mana buff above goes in, keep its health at 11.
